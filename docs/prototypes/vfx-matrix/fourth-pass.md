# Poison and Fighting VFX fourth pass

The [third review export](review-2026-10-12-round-3.json) has 12 approved,
40 needing work, and two unreviewed combinations. It provided focused notes
on five Poison and Fighting effects. Those five are refreshed in the
[live review grid](../../../web/vfx-review.html); the other 49 animations and
their marks carry forward.

| Combination | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| Fighting Physical | Removed the fist icon's cuff. A slightly smaller fist begins large and translucent at the tile center, rapidly shrinks into focus, and makes a small shake on impact. |
| Fighting Special | Reduced the orb's impact expansion and made its pressure rings proportionally larger. |
| Fighting Status | Removed the long forearms. Two fists move across one another into a compact guard. |
| Poison Physical | Replaced constant-width strokes with pointed, curved cut shapes that taper at both ends and widen in the middle. |
| Poison Special | Removed the falling drips and the orb's attached drip pixels. Bubbles now burst from the lower part of the tile. |

These are still development studies. The current pass changes no live battle
playback and does not revise the remaining first-pass effects. Review the five
new animations on the board before promoting them.
