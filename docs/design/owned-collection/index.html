Design notes & sources

Tap a Pokémon to inspect. Use Add, then choose a highlighted position. Existing members can be moved or removed through party Manage. Desktop: drag tiles or party members. Phone: hold the small grip to drag; tap is always available. Reload or Reset interactions restores the fixture. Search, sort and compact filters work on fixtures. Labels switch between Name, Combat power, IV perfection or None. Pages contain complete rows fitted to the viewport; Train is a future action.
