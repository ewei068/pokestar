Inspect a Pokémon in the collection, then press Train. Training sends one simulated request per second. Try Stop, a capped stat, or the Berry Farm. Confirmed stop rule: stop when neither EXP progression nor an EV change is possible at this location; level 100 counts as EXP complete, and Berry reductions count as EV changes. The real backend still stores EXP at level 100. Hidden tabs pause; returning does not resume automatically.
