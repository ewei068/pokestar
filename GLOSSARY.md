# Pokestar

Terms shared by battle rules and the interfaces through which players use them.

## Language

**Battle**:
A single combat run with an initial setup, participants, a sequence of actions, and an outcome. A battle has the same identity whether a participant views or continues it in Discord or on the web.

**Battle action**:
A choice made for the active Pokémon that advances a battle, currently using a move against a target or skipping a turn.

**Combat event**:
An observable occurrence during a battle action, such as a move starting, a target being missed, damage being taken, or an effect being applied. Combat events appear in the order they occurred.
